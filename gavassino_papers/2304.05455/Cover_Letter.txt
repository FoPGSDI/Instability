I am submitting the manuscript "Relativistic bulk viscous fluids of Burgers type", by L. Gavassino

The article uses rheological techniques to study the linear response properties of reactive mixtures in general relativity. The main goal is to understand what is the "correct" effective viscous theory that we should be using to describe neutron star matter.

It turns out that none of currently existing relativistic viscous theories is adequate. Instead, neutron star matter falls into a new class of bulk viscous fluids, which we call "of Burgers type". Such fluids are the relativistic generalization of a rheological model (the Burgers model) that is used in material engineering to describe certain types of viscoelastic materials.
