# LG Batch 1: Paper 5 -- Gavassino 2210.05067
## "Is Relativistic Hydrodynamics Always Symmetric-Hyperbolic in the Linear Regime?" (PRD 107, 065013, 2023)

### Status: INTEGRATED

### Key Result
Using the **Onsager-Casimir principle** (symmetry of kinetic coefficients under time reversal), the principal part of the linearised hydrodynamic equations is shown to be symmetric. Combined with thermodynamic stability, this implies **symmetric hyperbolicity** in the linear regime, provided no macroscopic magnetic fields or spins are present.

Applications: Carter's multifluid theory and IS in the pressure frame are proven symmetric-hyperbolic in the linear regime. Connection to GENERIC formalism established.
