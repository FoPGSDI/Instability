## "Fluctuating relativistic dissipative hydrodynamics as a gauge theory" (Ann. Phys. 442, 168902, 2022)

### Status: INTEGRATED

### Key Result
BDNK and IS are related by a **gauge transformation** (reparametrization of thermodynamic variables). The distinction between formalisms is analogous to gauge fixing in field theory. For bulk viscosity, the explicit mapping between BDNK frame corrections and IS relaxation variable Pi_bulk is constructed.

The "gauge" is the decomposition of T^munu into equilibrium + dissipative parts. Different decompositions (Landau, Eckart, BDNK general frame) are related by field redefinitions that leave the physical T^munu invariant.

### Physical Insight
This gauge perspective explains why BDNK and IS give identical physical predictions: they compute the same T^munu, just decomposed differently.
